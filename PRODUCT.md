# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Italian retail traders who already operate on MetaTrader 4/5: beginners who lose without understanding why, and experienced traders who want to automate position management and measure their own behaviour. Many trade prop-firm challenges, where one broken daily-loss rule ends the account.

## Product Purpose

Riskly sells risk management, not predictions. It gives traders the tools to decide risk before the trade, have rules enforced during it, and measure discipline after it. Success: the visitor understands that their losses come from management, not from wrong forecasts, and starts with a free tool, the journal trial, the course, or reserves a bot.

## Positioning

No signals, no strategies, no promise of profit. Every product protects the account or measures the trader; none tells you what to buy. Bots are paid once (lifetime licence), not a subscription.

## Operating Context

Products: four MetaTrader Expert Advisors (RiskGuard, TradeManager, ScaleIn, Riskly Pro) launching 23 October 2026; web journal at app.riskly.trade (7-day free trial, monthly / annual / lifetime plans); videocourse (47 lessons, 10 modules, ~3 hours, available now); five free browser calculators (position size per symbol, risk of ruin Monte Carlo, prop-firm margin, drawdown recovery, and more). Payments via Stripe; lead form and free PDF guide "Le 7 regole di rischio". Site is a single static HTML page (site/index.html) with client-side page switching, deployed to Netlify.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All copy is Italian. Prices and launch terms are fixed in the current site and must not change in a redesign: launch prices locked for life for the first 10 sign-ups; journal launch prices until 31 December. Legal disclaimer and privacy/terms sections are required. Calculators, forms, Stripe links, Supabase calls and page routing must keep working.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Riskly" and the logo (rising line over a base bar) are binding. Colours, typography and layout are open: the owner asked for a completely new visual world, not a variation of the current dark/lime look.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: product descriptions, prices, course outline, calculator logic, simulated bot demos, founder bio (Gabriele Liberto). Absent: customer testimonials, performance statistics, press. Never fabricate results, testimonials or profit claims.

## Product Principles

- Management over prediction: never imply the product picks trades.
- Show the mechanism working (calculators, simulations) instead of claiming outcomes.
- Plain numbers, honest limits: risks of trading stated clearly.
- Decide calm, execute automatically, measure afterwards.
